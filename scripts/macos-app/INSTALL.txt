  YIJING ORACLE — Installing on your Mac
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This app launches the Yijing oracle in its own window on your Mac.
No data is sent to the internet — everything runs locally on your
machine.

Because the app does not come from the App Store, the VERY FIRST
launch needs a few extra clicks. This is normal and only happens once.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 STEP 1 — Install
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Double-click "Yijing.dmg".
   A window opens with the "Yijing" icon and an "Applications"
   folder next to it.

2. Drag "Yijing" onto the "Applications" folder.
   That installs the app.

3. Eject the "Yijing Oracle" volume again: in the Finder, click the
   eject symbol next to "Yijing Oracle" (or drag the icon from the
   desktop to the Trash).


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 STEP 2 — The first launch (one time only)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Open the Applications folder and double-click "Yijing".

2. A notice appears saying the app cannot be opened because it is
   not from the App Store.
   → Click "Done" or "OK".
   → Do NOT click "Move to Trash"!

3. Open System Settings:
   Apple menu (  top left)  →  "System Settings…"

4. On the left, click "Privacy & Security".

5. Scroll all the way down on the right-hand side.
   It will say something like:
   ""Yijing" was blocked to protect your Mac."
   → Click the "Open Anyway" button next to it.

6. Confirm with your login password or Touch ID.

7. One last dialog appears.
   → Click "Open Anyway" there.

Done — the app opens in its own window.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 FROM THE SECOND LAUNCH ON
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Just double-click "Yijing" in the Applications folder — nothing else.
The steps from section 2 are never needed again.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 QUITTING THE APP
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Just close the window or press Cmd+Q. That fully quits the app.
