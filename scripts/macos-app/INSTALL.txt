  YIJING ORACLE — Installing on your Mac
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This app launches the Yijing oracle in its own window on your Mac.
No data is sent to the internet — everything runs locally on your
machine.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 STEP 1 — Install
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Double-click "Yijing.dmg".
   A window opens with the "Yijing" icon and an "Applications"
   folder next to it.

2. Drag "Yijing" onto the "Applications" folder.
   That installs the app.

3. Eject the "Yijing Oracle" volume again: in the Finder, click the
   eject symbol next to "Yijing Oracle" (or drag the icon from the
   desktop to the Trash).


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 STEP 2 — Launch
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Open the Applications folder and double-click "Yijing".
The app opens in its own window. That's it — nothing else to do.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 IF MACOS BLOCKS THE APP (un-notarized builds only)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Official releases are Apple-notarized and open without any warning.
You only need the steps below if you received an un-notarized build
and macOS says the app "cannot be opened because it is not from the
App Store". This is then needed once:

1. Click "Done" or "OK".  → Do NOT click "Move to Trash"!

2. Apple menu (  top left)  →  "System Settings…"

3. On the left, click "Privacy & Security".

4. Scroll all the way down on the right-hand side. It will say
   something like:  ""Yijing" was blocked to protect your Mac."
   → Click the "Open Anyway" button next to it.

5. Confirm with your login password or Touch ID, then click
   "Open Anyway" once more.

Done — the app opens, and this is never needed again.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 QUITTING THE APP
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Just close the window or press Cmd+Q. That fully quits the app.
